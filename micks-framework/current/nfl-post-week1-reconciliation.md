# Micks Picks NFL Post-Week 1 Reconciliation Module

Effective: 2026-09-17  
Status: Permanent active module for Week 2 forward

## Governing rule

Every NFL side, moneyline, total, player prop, derivative and parlay leg must complete the guide/system reconciliation below **before** its final Micks score, grade or unit size is assigned.

The stored 2026 VSiN NFL Betting Guide and Fantasy Life Fantasy Football 2026 magazine are preseason priors. Current weekly projections, verified Week 1 usage, injuries, depth charts and executable prices are the live evidence. A guide can establish an expectation; it cannot overrule current verified information or create a bet by itself.

Recovery Mode+ remains controlling:

- minimum official score: 82/110
- minimum Failure Score: 7/10
- maximum official plays: 1–2
- no forced release
- exact price must remain inside the No-Bet Cutoff

## Mandatory evidence lanes

### 1. Stored guide priors

Record the applicable preseason expectation from:

- **VSiN 2026 NFL Betting Guide:** team power rating, projected line/value, coaching or scheme change, schedule, regression and market strategy.
- **Fantasy Life Fantasy Football 2026:** projected plays, dropback rate, touchdowns, offensive-line grade, depth chart, player volume, route/target/carry expectation, goal-line role and utilization notes.

Mark each applicable guide assumption as `CONFIRMED`, `PARTIALLY CURRENT`, `STALE` or `OVERRIDDEN`.

### 2. Current weekly prediction layer

Check and timestamp the newest available:

- Fantasy Life weekly projections and rankings
- Fantasy Life Utilization Report, snap counts and air-yards data
- Fantasy Life game-by-game analysis and role upgrades/downgrades
- VSiN NFL game projections, Makinen power ratings, injury report and matchup ratings
- VSiN NFL prop projections/prop analyzer and WR/CB matchup tool when applicable
- current VSiN Week 2+ betting systems and splits
- at least one independent non-guide statistical source when available

The Fantasy Life magazine, current Fantasy Life projections, rankings and Utilization Report are one source family. The VSiN guide, VSiN current models and VSiN systems are one source family. Do not count multiple pages from one family as multiple independent confirmations.

### 3. Observed current-season evidence

Use Week 1 and then cumulative current-season data, with garbage time, injuries, weather and unusual game states removed or flagged.

Team evidence:

- neutral-script pace and pass rate over expectation
- plays, drives and seconds per snap
- early-down pass rate and no-huddle usage
- EPA, success rate and yards per play
- pressure created/allowed and pass-block/run-block performance
- explosive pass/run rate
- third-down, red-zone and points-per-drive performance
- personnel grouping, motion and play-action changes
- offensive-line combinations and defensive rotations

Player evidence:

- snaps and snap share
- routes and route participation per team dropback
- targets, target share, first-read share and targets per route
- air-yard share and average depth of target
- carries, rush share and designed quarterback runs
- two-minute, third-down, short-yardage and goal-line role
- red-zone/end-zone opportunities
- workload limitations, substitutions and injury effects

Opportunity and role are more predictive early in the season than touchdowns, fantasy points or efficiency from a small sample.

## Weeks 2–4 blending matrix

These are starting weights, not permission to average away a known structural change.

| Data class | Week 2: preseason/current | Weeks 3–4: preseason/current | Rule |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Player opportunity and role | 40% / 60% | 25% / 75% | Full-game snaps, routes and high-value touches can change a role quickly. |
| Team pace, pass rate and play volume | 65% / 35% | 45% / 55% | One game is noisy; verified play-caller or personnel changes can override. |
| EPA, yards/play and scoring efficiency | 75% / 25% | 55% / 45% | Regress one-game efficiency heavily. |
| OL/pass-rush/coverage performance | 50% / 50% | 35% / 65% | Current injuries and lineup changes override the numerical blend. |

Reduce the current-season weight when Week 1 contained an early injury, extreme weather, overtime, a major blowout, unusual defensive/special-teams scores or a player with an incomplete role sample.

Current weekly projections are a comparison output, not another independent weighting bucket. Record their change from the magazine baseline and determine what information caused the change.

## Week 1 adjustment controls

### Team power ratings

- Normal Week 1 adjustment: 0.25 to 1.0 point.
- More than 1.0 point requires a verified structural reason such as quarterback change, multiple offensive-line injuries, major coverage/pass-rush loss, clear scheme failure or a role-changing personnel shift.
- Without a quarterback change or equivalent structural event, cap a one-week move at 1.5 points.
- Do not upgrade or downgrade from final score alone.

### Sides and totals

- Build the new Micks line/total before reading current expert picks.
- Separate sustainable pace/efficiency from turnovers, defensive scores, short fields and red-zone variance.
- Increase trench weight: pass protection, pressure without blitzing, run push and line health must be explicit.
- Name the players and roles that create the expected game script.
- When the VSiN guide/system, Fantasy Life current projection and market disagree, record the conflict; do not average it away.

### Player props

Rebuild the projection from opportunity first:

`updated opportunity × regressed efficiency = updated median projection`

- Quarterbacks: dropbacks, attempts, sacks, scrambles and designed runs.
- Running backs: rush share, route share, target share, two-minute and goal-line roles.
- Wide receivers/tight ends: route participation, target/first-read share, air yards and red-zone/end-zone use.
- Touchdowns, broken plays and one-game yards per touch/reception receive the least weight.
- Use median and distribution, not a ceiling outcome, to compare with the sportsbook threshold.

A prop based mainly on a new one-game role is capped at 0.50u. It may reach 0.75u only when the guide prior, verified usage, current weekly projection, matchup and price all align.

If Week 1 usage contradicts the Fantasy Life magazine and the current weekly Fantasy Life projection does not clearly resolve the contradiction, the prop is `WATCH/PASS`.

## VSiN and historical system controls

Week 2 overreaction, blowout, favorite/underdog, divisional and totals systems are candidate filters only.

For every system used, record:

- exact qualifying rule
- sample period and sample size
- ATS/OU record and price assumption when supplied
- plausible football mechanism
- whether the current roster, quarterback, coaching staff and market number still fit

All VSiN systems occupy one supporting-system lane. Do not system-shop until a candidate agrees, and do not add system credit on top of Progressive Fade or market points for the same fact.

## Mandatory reconciliation record

Every serious NFL candidate must contain:

- market and exact executable price
- VSiN guide prior
- Fantasy Life magazine prior
- Week 1/current usage and efficiency evidence
- current Fantasy Life projection/ranking/utilization update
- current VSiN model/system position
- Micks updated projection and fair number
- source-family count
- guide status: `CONFIRMED`, `PARTIALLY CURRENT`, `STALE` or `OVERRIDDEN`
- strongest agreement and strongest contradiction
- reason for every material projection change
- Best Number and No-Bet Cutoff
- Failure Score and final disposition

An unresolved material contradiction caps the candidate at 81/110 and blocks an official Recovery Mode+ release.

## Required full-scan output

Every full NFL scan or rerun must show:

1. Guide/System Reconciliation Table
2. Fantasy Projection Delta Board
3. Updated Micks power-rating/total changes
4. Scored sides, totals, props and derivatives
5. Added candidates and why they appeared
6. Removed candidates and what invalidated them

No NFL scan may be called complete when the Fantasy Life magazine prior, current Fantasy Life weekly layer or current VSiN guide/system layer was skipped, unavailable without disclosure, or not reconciled against verified current data.
