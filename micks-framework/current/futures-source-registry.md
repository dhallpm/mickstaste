# Micks Picks Futures Lab Source Registry

Use only sources that are current and applicable to the market being modeled. A source may inform probability, calibration, market price, role/health, or scenario assumptions; no single outside model creates a futures release.

## Market / price / movement
- VSiN and Circa market information
- Action Network futures odds, movement, consensus and analysis
- Covers futures/consensus pages
- Multiple major sportsbook boards and user-provided executable screenshots
- Doc's Sports, including dynamic AI-v3 page, supporting only

- Official NFL/team injury, roster and depth-chart sources

## Awards and player futures
Use sport-specific playing-time, usage, performance and team-success models plus historical award-voting structure where relevant. Narrative-based articles are context only.

## Required source handling
- Mark sources CURRENT, STALE, INACCESSIBLE, NOT APPLICABLE, or SUPPORTING ONLY.
- Never invent a projection or price when a source is inaccessible.
- Prefer independent models that use different methodologies; three sites repeating the same market consensus are not three model paths.
- Current roster/health/role information overrides stale preseason-guide assumptions.
- Always line shop longshot and outright futures because price dispersion can dominate the edge calculation.
