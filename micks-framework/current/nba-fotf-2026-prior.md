## Micks Picks integration

1. **NBA preseason team priors:** Use tier, direction and panel disagreement as background checks on roster durability, star-age window, asset flexibility, management and rebuild/tank incentives. Investigate the current cause before adjusting a preseason strength estimate. Do not convert rank positions directly into points, win probabilities, ATS edges, team totals or daily model weights.
2. **Daily game predictions:** Current player availability, starting lineup, minutes, pace, efficiency, rest and market number outrank this ten-year prior. It can generate questions (rotation incentives, window urgency, development) and flag a questionable long-term assumption; it earns no automatic 110-point score or independent confirmation by itself. Do not infer a team's motivation in one game solely from its ranking.
3. **Futures Lab:** Compare the long-horizon ranking with current title, conference, playoff and season-win markets. For a **2026-27 title** future, build a separate one-season Micks probability, path simulation, de-vigged consensus and EV under futures-lab.md. The ranking may support a structural scenario and challenge an extreme price; it is not a direct ten-season-to-one-season conversion. Discount disagreement-heavy teams and check portfolio correlation.
