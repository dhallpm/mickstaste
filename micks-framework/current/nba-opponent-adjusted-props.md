# NBA opponent-adjusted player-prop analysis
Effective November 5, 2026. User-directed correction. Applies to Run Vegas Picks, Run Micks Picks, Spurs spreadsheet prop reviews and every NBA prop parlay leg.

## Required order
1. Identify opponent weaknesses relevant to the exact statistic and the player's actual role.
2. Verify likely defenders, available frontcourt/backcourt rotation, injuries and matchup exposure.
3. Establish projected minutes and opportunities, then a matchup-adjusted statistical projection.
4. Compare the projection with the exact offered threshold and odds.
5. Validate with spreadsheet distributions and counterexamples.
6. Score using existing 110-point factors and Failure Score. Preserve existing grade ladders, consideration overrides and release rules.

Season averages, recent hit rates, prior opponent results and a simple minutes-times-average calculation cannot alone establish a matchup edge or qualify a prop. A dated season guide is a prior, not tonight's player projection. No new requirement to invent a win probability, calibrated model or numerical EV is introduced.

## Required candidate record
- Event/date/start time; player, exact market, threshold and book odds.
- Role: listed position, actual offensive role, probable assignment and material role changes. Examine adjacent position groups when relevant. Wembanyama's F-C label or Harper's guard/wing deployment must not be silently reduced to one broad group.
- Opponent allowance for the selected statistic against the relevant position/role, rank direction, league benchmark, sample games, minutes/possessions normalization and source/as-of time. Separate season and recent windows. Early-season samples need opponent-quality and schedule context.
- Individual defense: likely primary and secondary defenders, expected rotation/minutes, relevant rim/shot-zone/defender evidence and uncertainty. Use historical official matchups as supporting evidence only. Do not call a defender weak from size, reputation or one poor result alone.
- Opportunity: projected minutes with rationale, usage/shot attempts and zones for points; rebound chances and available misses for rebounds; potential assists, passing role and teammate conversion for assists; attempts and shot location for threes. Distinguish verified from estimated inputs.
- Game conditions: possessions, rest/travel, injuries, expected rotation, foul risk and blowout risk. A favorable defender matchup can improve production per minute; it does not independently prove more minutes.
- External projections: seek at least two independent current providers; record each accessible player's minutes and separate PTS/REB/AST/3PM outputs, timestamp, injury assumptions and model family. DFS fantasy-point totals are not points, PRA or a prop probability. Disclose inaccessible providers; source count alone never creates advantage.
- Micks projection: named method, baseline, opponent/role/minute adjustments, output or justified range, assumptions and uncertainty. Do not invent adjustment percentages, average incompatible models or treat a market-implied score as an independent forecast. A model-based point estimate is required for a model-based claim; a qualitative provisional review may remain unscored when inputs are unavailable.
- Price: compare the exact stat projection to the offered line and odds, explain the cashing and failure paths, and establish Best Number/cutoff under existing rules. A mean above a threshold does not establish betting value without considering distribution and price.
- Workbook validation: current sample size, threshold hit count, minutes distribution, same-opponent context and outliers. Separate actuals from pregame forecasts and preserve pregame snapshots after the game.
- Contradiction and disposition: strongest contrary matchup/projection, missing core inputs, factor assessment and WATCH/UNGRADED/PASS/release status.

## Mandatory sources and interpretation
- NBA.com official injury reports, advanced, tracking, matchups and defensive play-type data. Use relevant player matchups/shot zones rather than infer coverage calls.
- TeamRankings opponent paint points, two-point/effective shooting, rebounds, assists, pace and relevant home/road/recent splits. Team-level rates support environment; they are not defense-vs-position values or individual-defender grades.
- FantasyPros defense-vs-position and RotoWire defense-vs-position/projections, when accessible. Verify position filter, season, date, scoring and rank direction from table structure. Repeated unlabeled mobile/desktop rows are not separately verified positions.
- Current independent stat projections from registered VSiN/Opta, RotoWire, FantasyPros, Statz, DraftKings-related DFS providers or other verifiable providers. Try lawful alternatives for inaccessible tables. NFL-only Fantasy Life materials are NOT APPLICABLE to NBA.
- The Rundown (therundown.gg) opponent and player context; retain the exact window and distinguish cumulative snapshots from game-specific data.
- VSiN original guide pages plus current guide/model reconciliation; Doc's, Action, Covers, StatMuse and Sports Reference remain required according to the existing source registry. Keep source-family deduplication.

Missing a paid source is not an automatic veto when independent accessible evidence establishes the core handicap. Missing meaningful opponent/role/opportunity analysis is a core gap: retain as WATCH/UNGRADED instead of recommending from hit rate alone. Do not award matchup or personnel points merely for opening a page.

## Required display
Each serious prop gets a compact row:
Player / prop and odds | opponent position/role allowance and sample | likely defender | minutes and opportunity | external projections | Micks adjustment/output | workbook hit rate | countercase | status.

Writeups must explain why this opponent changes expected production. If no defensible change is supported, say neutral matchup. A favorable team defense statistic must not automatically boost every Spurs prop.

## Postgame audit
Record actual minutes, opportunities, matchup exposure and outcome separately. Classify a missed matchup input as a process error, not ordinary shooting variance. Do not retrospectively claim a 52-point outcome was the pregame expectation, or use the outcome to label every defender weak. Preserve historical published tickets and grades.
