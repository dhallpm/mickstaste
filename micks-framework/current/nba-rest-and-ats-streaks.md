
## Mandatory back-to-back and ATS streak checks — October 24, 2026

Applies to every NBA Micks Picks scan and Vegas Picks run, including straight sides, totals, props, ordinary parlay legs and seven-point teaser legs.

### Schedule flags
- Flag each team separately as B2B-1 (playing today and tomorrow), B2B-2 (played yesterday and playing today), or NOT B2B, using NBA schedule dates in the venue's local calendar and an ET display date. Do not equate elapsed hours with rest days.
- Show both teams' rest days, previous opponent/date/venue, previous starter minutes, overtime, home/away sequence (H-H, H-A, A-H, A-A), travel and next game. Unknown minutes/travel remain UNKNOWN. Check three games in four nights and four in six as additional workload flags.
- B2B-2 is a review flag, not an automatic fade, Under, player scratch or score deduction. Both teams on B2B does not imply equal workload. Evaluate actual fatigue, minutes, rotation changes and whether the price already reflects the schedule.
- Reconcile the original season-guide premise, current official injury report and team reporting. Any material unresolved rest/minutes decision holds the affected market; do not transfer yesterday's availability into today.
- Carry these flags onto the ranked board, each standalone writeup, parlay/teaser leg and ticket's shared-risk summary.

### Five-game ATS streak flags
- Mandatory sources: TeamRankings current NBA ATS trends AND chronological team results/closing lines; cross-check relevant game logs with Covers, VSiN or another independently sourced outlet. A season aggregate or a filtered trend such as 5-0 on Tuesdays does not establish five consecutive overall covers.
- For every team, show current-season overall ATS record, last-five chronological full-game ATS outcomes, active consecutive streak length and exact dates/opponents/closing spreads/final scores for any flag.
- Emit ATS COVER 5+ for at least five uninterrupted covers and ATS MISS 5+ for at least five uninterrupted failures to cover. Continue displaying the actual length at six, seven and beyond; a 4-of-5 record does not qualify.
- Grade from the team's perspective: team final margin plus its closing spread >0 = cover, <0 = miss, =0 = push. A push breaks the literal consecutive-cover/consecutive-miss flag; display it explicitly. Cancelled games/voids are excluded; a missing closing line makes the sequence UNKNOWN rather than a guessed result.
- Primary streak scope: current-season NBA regular season, reset at the new season; exclude preseason, Summer League and non-standings exhibitions. With fewer than five completed games report INSUFFICIENT CURRENT-SEASON GAMES, not a five-game streak.
- Also examine the latest five eligible games across the season boundary and label any verified carryover separately as CROSS-SEASON; regular-season-only and playoff-inclusive sequences must be separate and explicit. Never blend them silently. If historical prices cannot be verified, report CARRYOVER UNKNOWN.
- TeamRankings/cross-source disagreements around a closing half point must remain visible with each provider's line; do not cherry-pick the close to create a streak.
- Track standard full-game closing-spread ATS separately from performance against our selected seven-point teaser thresholds. A five-game ATS streak is not a five-game teaser record.
- A hot/cold ATS streak triggers research into opponent strength, roster changes, margin vs close, shooting variance and possible favorite inflation/progressive fade. It creates neither a pick nor an automatic due-to-cover/fade claim.
