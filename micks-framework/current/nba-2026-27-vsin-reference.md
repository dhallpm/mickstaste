## Mandatory per-candidate reconciliation

1. Identify both teams and the exact player/market/line/book/time. Read the relevant team pages and page 13; for futures or awards also read pages 4-12 when applicable. For each parlay leg, perform its own check. For season-long player awards, identify the player's current team rather than invent an opponent.

2. Compare the guide's applicable thesis with current official roster/coaching/injury/lineup evidence, projected and recent minutes/usage, pace, efficiency, shot mix, rest/travel, current price and current VSiN/Doc's positions. Record both support and the strongest countercase. A historical win-total opinion is not daily ATS confirmation.

3. Attach `NBA Guide Check` using the JSON fields. `basis` is `ORIGINAL_PAGES` only when those pages were actually read; otherwise `EXTRACTED_BASELINE`. A runtime with only this supplied digest must say so. Record PDF pages, the specific guide finding, current finding, actual current source URLs, check timestamp, freshness state, influence on the pick and material-conflict resolution. Never fabricate a retrieval. If a missing original passage is material, retrieve it from Library or keep the candidate WATCH/BLOCKED.

4. CURRENT means the applicable assumption was confirmed now, not that a season guide became a current feed. PARTIALLY_CURRENT isolates what still holds. STALE gets no current-evidence credit. OVERRIDDEN explicitly replaces the old assumption with verified evidence. An override may resolve the conflict; it must not be concealed. Missing guide reconciliation or an unresolved material contradiction blocks official release; no arbitrary score deduction or bonus is created.

5. Before release, repeat the established 45-minute news/price recheck where applicable. A current quote alone does not establish role or minutes. Update changed team/rotation assumptions after transactions, injury restrictions, coaching changes or meaningful role evidence. Early-season structural priors can inform uncertainty; current evidence increasingly dominates. Do not invent fixed blend weights.

## New analytical checks from the guide

- Use source power ratings as a dated comparison baseline, not a direct current point spread or calibrated win probability.
- Reconcile pace, half-court creation, rim/three-point shot mix, offensive rebounding, turnovers and foul/free-throw environment; separate shooting variance from opportunity.
- Confirm current roster/coaching/starting lineup, minutes ramp, usage redistribution and rest/travel; active does not mean unrestricted.
- Treat 2025-26 adjusted metrics as historical snapshots; the guide does not establish the exact filters or sample dates. Printed SU/ATS/O-U records can include postseason games.
- Preserve each author and exact threshold for win totals/futures. A published lean is not proof of an executed position.
- For incentive scenarios, verify applicable official lottery rules and actual team incentives; never adopt alleged rule changes or allegations as confirmed facts from the guide alone.
- For awards, evaluate role/minutes, plausible improvement, voter narratives and current official eligibility; age/experience patterns are heuristics, not hard exclusions.
- Reconcile Silver Bulletin ten-season franchise priors separately from one-season VSiN views; never average incompatible horizons.
- Keep v2 110-point weights and current NBA release modes. Guides inform existing factors without bonus points or new standalone probability/EV prerequisites.
